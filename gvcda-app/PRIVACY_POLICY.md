# Privacy Policy — GVCDA App

**Last updated:** [DATE]

This Privacy Policy describes how the GVCDA app ("we", "us", "the app") collects,
uses, and protects information from Members, Employees, and Retailers who use it.

## Information We Collect

- **Phone number and password** — used to create your account and log you in. Your
  password is stored only as a one-way cryptographic hash (bcrypt) — we cannot see
  or recover your actual password.
- **Name and profile details** — full name, age, gender (optional), address, and
  the District/Mandal/Village you select, used to personalize your experience
  (e.g. showing retailers and jobs near you).
- **Location (GPS)** — collected only from Employee accounts, only while the app is
  open, and only to log field visits (enrolling members/retailers in person). We do
  not track location in the background, and Members/Retailers are never asked for
  GPS location.
- **Photos** — Retailers may upload storefront and product photos, stored securely
  and shown to Members browsing the catalogue.
- **Order and transaction information** — items ordered, order status, and (for
  membership purchases and retailer commission settlements) a payment reference
  code (UTR) you report after paying via bank transfer/UPI. We do not collect or
  store your bank card numbers, UPI PIN, or any banking credentials — payments are
  made directly through your own banking/UPI app, not through us.
- **Retailer business details** — for Retailer accounts: business name, category,
  bank account/UPI ID for commission settlement, GST number (optional).
- **Complaints and job applications** — text you submit when raising a complaint or
  applying to a job posting.

## How We Use This Information

- To operate the core features of the app: membership management, retailer
  catalogues and ordering, job listings, complaint handling, and employee field
  operations.
- To verify payments: an Admin manually checks the payment reference (UTR) you
  submit against the actual bank statement before a membership or commission
  settlement is marked as paid.
- To calculate commissions, incentives, and membership benefits.
- We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not sell your
  information to third parties.

## Data Sharing

- Retailer business details (name, category, location, products) are shown to
  Members browsing the catalogue — this is a public-facing part of the app by
  design.
- We do not share your phone number, address, or payment reference codes with any
  third party, except as required by law.
- Photos are stored with Supabase (our cloud storage provider) and served
  directly to the app — Supabase does not use this data for any purpose of its own.

## Data Retention

We retain your account information for as long as your account is active. You may
request account deactivation or data deletion by contacting us (see below).

## Your Choices

- You can update your profile information from within the app.
- You can change your password at any time from within the app.
- You can request that your account be deactivated or your data deleted by
  contacting us at the email below.

## Children's Privacy

This app is not directed at children under 18 and we do not knowingly collect
information from children.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Continued use of the app after a
change means you accept the updated policy.
